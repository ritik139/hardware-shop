Frequently asked

Which finishes are available?

Every Korraz piece comes in six premium PVD finishes: Polished Gold, Antique Brass, Rose Gold, Matte Black, Satin Nickel and Antique Copper. Availability can vary by product.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We ship from Mumbai to every part of India with safe, secure packaging.

How do I get pricing?

Tap Enquire on WhatsApp with the product, size and finish you want, and our team will confirm current pricing and availability.

Is the hardware solid brass?

Yes. Our fittings are cast in solid brass and PVD coated so they resist tarnish and corrosion.
